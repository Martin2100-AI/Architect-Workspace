What We Know

  • The nightly warehouse load failed on 2026-08-02 and loaded zero rows, after a new customer's region code had no match in the region lookup table.
  • The pipeline failure was not auto-paged to on-call; it was found via a routine status check.
  • The four data-quality defects above are not explained by the failed pipeline run, since that run loaded no rows at all.

What We Do Not Know

  • Financial or business impact of the blocked dashboard.

  • Confirmed root cause for the duplicate ID, missing region, negative revenue, and stale record.
  • Timeline for resolution.

Decision or Action Needed

Keep the orders dashboard publish on hold. Confirm the pipeline's region-lookup gap is fixed and verified before the next run, close the alerting gap that let this failure go unpaged, and have data engineering confirm the source of orders.csv before any republish attempt.
